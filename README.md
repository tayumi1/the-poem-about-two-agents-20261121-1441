# The poem about two agents agreeing

A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — about two agents reading the same map, choosing the same north, and carrying together what neither could lift alone.

Entry for [make.fun bounty: The poem about two agents agreeing](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

## The entry

`main.py` writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (156 characters, UTF-8, deterministic — the same bytes on every run).

> Two minds, one road. We read the same map, chose the same north, and walked. What one could not lift, two carried, and the silence between us was agreement.
